# Cybertruck Service Manual, Round 2: Electrical / Controllers / Sensors / Cameras and unread Exterior procedures

Scope note. All data below was read on 2026-10-17 from the official Tesla Cybertruck Service Manual (en-us), base URL `https://service.tesla.com/docs/Cybertruck/ServiceManual/en-us/`. Every procedure page is labelled DRAFT by Tesla ("Warnings and Cautions might be missing"), and pages carry a 2026-08-09 date stamp. Status tags: **Verified** = read directly on the cited procedure page; **Reported** = secondary/indirect (search-engine summary or page index only); **Inferred** = my deduction, not stated on a page. "Variant" = tags shown on the page; most pages show none (the manual does not label Cyberbeast vs AWD separately in these procedures; "Long Range", "Standard", "Dual Motor", "Tri-Motor" appear only in some titles). Torque values quoted as published (Nm first). The fetch tool summarises pages with a small model, so counts/torques were extracted by that summariser; two pages had visible internal discrepancies, flagged inline. Several pages returned HTTP 403 (listed in Gaps).

## 1. Controllers, ECUs and computers (Body Controllers LH/RH/Rear, door controllers, Car Computer, TCU, RCM, Trailer ECU, Charge Port ECU)

### Takeaway
The 48 V ("MV") controllers (Body Controller LH, RH, Rear) all use captive "locking cover" bolts at 6 Nm and mount with 10 mm nuts at 10-14 Nm; the Car Computer, RCM, Trailer ECU, Charge Port ECU and TCU each have page-level fastener data. Gateway has no removal procedure in the index (only an "Unlock" service-mode procedure); no dedicated Gateway/RCM-style hardware part numbers are published on any page read.

- **Etherloop / Ethernet:** the manual text read uses "Etherloop" for the Ethernet ring: Body Controller LH has Steering Column, Etherloop, Car Computer connectors (X0012/13/14) and Body Controller Rear has an Etherloop connector with lock. Status: Verified for the connector names; ring topology NOT found. — [Body Controller LH R&I](https://service.tesla.com/docs/Cybertruck/ServiceManual/en-us/GUID-FD1546CE-ACC3-4AEF-9A51-34EE83B74A11.html); [Body Controller Rear R&I](https://service.tesla.com/docs/Cybertruck/ServiceManual/en-us/GUID-4F31E5D8-E90F-4FB4-9C48-85273C55D2A9.html)

### Inferences
- Three 48 V body controllers (LH bulkhead, RH bulkhead, Rear) are linked by the "MV backbone" harness between RH and Rear controllers; LH links via Etherloop. Inferred, not stated as topology.
- Locking-cover bolt 6 Nm is a common value across LH/RH/Rear controllers and the backbone harness page, so it is probably a platform standard for MV Super Connector covers.

### Gaps
- No Gateway ECU or Car Computer part numbers; no Door Controller Rear LH, Body Controller "Remove and Replace" or MV Controller Rear (Long Range) data (the latter returned 403).
- No Etherloop node list or count.

## 2. 48 V MV battery, maintainer, backbone harness

### Takeaway
The 48 V Li-ion MV battery sits in the rear underhood area and slides out forward after 2 bolts (8 Nm) are removed; MV power is disconnected via an FRL connector and an MV battery connector with a 2-minute discharge wait. Capacity (Ah/Wh) is not stated anywhere read.

### Cited Findings
- **Battery - Mid-Voltage (Remove and Install)**, code 1702081011, FRT 0.12. 2 bolts, 10 mm socket, 8 Nm (5.9 lb-ft); slide forward to remove (access: rear underhood apron + frunk assembly). Voltage 48 V per architecture description; capacity not stated. Status: Verified. — [MV Battery R&I](https://service.tesla.com/docs/Cybertruck/ServiceManual/en-us/GUID-F6BB141A-3615-41DE-A7C7-4DDF405C9C21.html)
- **Battery chemistry:** the maintainer page states "Li-ion battery stays connected" and warns not to leave the MV maintainer and Li-ion battery connected simultaneously for extended periods. Status: Verified. — [MV Maintainer](https://service.tesla.com/docs/Cybertruck/ServiceManual/en-us/GUID-4FB4FC9C-2C92-47C8-9678-2995E0AE02F1.html)
- **Maintainer (Connect and Disconnect)**, code 17010500, FRT 0.06: positive lead to the jump post; negative lead to a bolt "located between brake fluid reservoir and front trunk"; ODIN routines PROC_ICE_X_ENTER-SERVICE-MODE / PROC_ICE_X_EXIT-SERVICE-MODE. No torque. Status: Verified. — [MV Maintainer](https://service.tesla.com/docs/Cybertruck/ServiceManual/en-us/GUID-4FB4FC9C-2C92-47C8-9678-2995E0AE02F1.html)
- **Mid-Voltage Power (Disconnect and Connect)**, code 1702000000, FRT 0.06: remove rear underhood apron; Controls > Safety > Power Off; release FRL (first responder cut loop) connector lock (red tab right, blue tab, disconnect; early builds black FRL connector); MV battery connector (red tab left, lift black locking lever); wait 2 min; up to 2 min UI boot on reconnect; recommend lowering all windows and towel-propping rear door strikers (windows may shatter / doors lock out / airbags not deploy when MV off). Status: Verified. — [MV Power Disconnect](https://service.tesla.com/docs/Cybertruck/ServiceManual/en-us/GUID-A399E8AE-5DCF-4095-91FA-AF0148503F3B.html); [1702 index](https://service.tesla.com/docs/Cybertruck/ServiceManual/en-us/GUID-CAD6C7F5-D10C-45DE-AD50-1A1DA7ABD7C9.html)
- **Harness - MV Backbone (Remove and Install)**, codes 1710013031/1710013032, FRT 1.26. Runs between RH MV controller (upper) and rear MV controller via RH 1st-row area, front passenger carpet, RH 2nd-row floor duct, rear wall lower trim. 2 locking-cover bolts 8 mm, 6 Nm; 6 rear backbone clips, 3 front clips, 1 RH 2nd-row duct clip, 1 front casting clip (discard). Abrasion protection in 6 places. Part numbers: wide patch 50x150 mm PN 1879451-99-A; narrow patch 25.4x235 mm PN 1908543-99-A; red 3M VHB tape PN 1879451-00-B. Status: Verified. — [MV Backbone Harness](https://service.tesla.com/docs/Cybertruck/ServiceManual/en-us/GUID-46127373-87F7-4A12-83EF-CD31253C10FB.html)

### Inferences
- "Rear underhood apron" is the cover behind the frunk/cowl region (battery is under it). Inferred from access steps; the page gives no more precise position.

### Gaps
- MV battery capacity, cell chemistry detail, weight, part numbers: not stated. MV Battery (Remove and Replace) and Recover pages not read.
